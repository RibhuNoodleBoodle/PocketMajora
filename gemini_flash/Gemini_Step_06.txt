         `sceKernelDelayThread`. If a frame ever runs late (`wait_usec < 0`), rebase
         `next_usec = now_usec` immediately (NO catch-up spiral!).

         2) Run `GameState::update()` once (`20 Hz`) and record transforms in `FrameInterpolator`.

            `GfxMasterList` through `pmm-gfx` with `fraction = 0.5` interpolated matrices

            `GfxMasterList` through `pmm-gfx` with `fraction = 1.0` authoritative matrices
